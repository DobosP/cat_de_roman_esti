### 4.6 Trust pin (planned wait)

1. Append `GATE PENDING <target> sha=<sha> | untrusted gate files | <UTC>` to `GATE_REQUESTS.md` and to Progress, and commit.

3. Then run the pending gate on HEAD. A clean pass on a later HEAD closes every earlier milestone whose gate was pending; record that in Progress.
