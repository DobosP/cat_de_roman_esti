# ADR-0151: Expand game vocabulary and search earned recipes

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-15
- Extends [ADR-0150](0150-expand-vocabulary-and-clarify-game-controls.md).

## Decision

The owner requested a fresh words and game-quality session after V92 landed. V93 is a
bounded pass from `bf9d814` on `feat/v93-words-and-game-quality`, following the outcome
and review requirements of ADR-0113 and the critique rubric.

Add thirteen reviewed rounds/targets: one Conexiuni, two Cald sau Rece, two Lanț, four
Intrusul and four Perechi. New pack records pass exact independent raw and allocated-ID
gates; authored quick boards pass complete semantic reviews, original mechanics/rating
checks and final natural-selection BFF audits. Reject sense ambiguity, recycled groups
and misleading associative feedback even when structural floors pass. All previous
pack and quick payloads remain exact; private ranks and selection weights recompute.

Expand Alchimie to 235 concepts and 315 recipes with ten discoverable preparations and
21 recipes. Prefer meaningful alternate approaches and onward uses of existing results.
Four formerly terminal discoveries become ingredients; the new Pesmet leads to existing
Șnițel. Preserve all 225 prior concept records and 294 recipes, the supply/goal structure,
capacity bounds, provenance and five prior saved-book generations. New world-local
words must not shadow an existing shared KG identity or alias. Every concept/recipe
has complete independent coverage; final approvals bind the exact catalog and live audit.

Make the growing earned-recipe journal searchable by result or either ingredient.
Search ignores Romanian accents and case, operates locally on earned server-returned
rows, and preserves game selection and saved progress. An empty result offers immediate
reset; Escape also clears the query. A closed journal shows at most two lines of its
query while the editable field retains the full text. Recipe/source controls have 44px
touch targets. Hard-mode Contexto copy describes harder associations, matching the
rubric's recognition floor rather than promising obscurity.

## Evidence and limits

[The review archive](../reviews/v93-words-and-game-quality/README.md) preserves candidates,
independent factual/quality decisions, primary references, author exclusions, natural
BFF wins, recovery checks and GUI captures. The Maiorescu leadership concern was resolved
against the journal's own account, distinguishing broad leadership from a formal editor
title. Recipe references were repaired before final review; noodle-soup preparation was
corrected to respect the existing cooked-pasta input.

An exact outer inverse preserves all earlier five-artifact historical snapshots and
assertions. The original V92 world is reconstructed exactly for its historical tests;
new tests exercise the expanded installed world and old saves. Final integrated results
and exact inventory belong in [STATUS](../STATUS.md). Automated and agent reviews do not
establish human enjoyment or physical-device acceptance. Main publication, production
deployment and an automatic next session are not part of this content session.
