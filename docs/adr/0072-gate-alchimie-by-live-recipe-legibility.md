# ADR-0072: Gate Alchimie by live recipe legibility

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-01

## Context

ADR-0065 left 21 Alchimie records pending. The fresh V48 deterministic critique reported
zero failures and only three salience warnings because its craft profile traversed the
broad source graph. Alchimie players receive a smaller private, target-useful recipe
projection. The broad dossiers counted 158 productive seed pairs; only 50 survive in the
live projections, and seven boards fall from at least three apparent openings to one.

Broad closure and opening counts therefore cannot establish E2 intuitive openings or E5
bounded choice. Reusing V42 judgments would not solve the mismatch: their bindings are
stale against the current pack, KG, rubric, and sparse-recipe runtime. Empty shelves are
not a release quota.

## Decision

1. Require every Alchimie pending gate to carry an exact, source-bound live sparse
   projection artifact. Bind the sorted IDs, archived source records and record digests,
   pre-apply pack, KG, rubric, dossiers, runtime sources, and generator. Record exact par,
   opening pairs, recipes, routes, projected concepts, and all runtime bounds. Both
   independent E1–E5 judgments bind the same artifact digest; stale or tampered evidence
   fails closed.
2. Continue conservative synthesis: two promotions are required to ship; either rejection
   rejects; ADR-0019/A5 alcohol-dependent play remains pending for owner disposition.
   Broad graph statistics remain diagnostic evidence, not a substitute for simulating the
   recipe book served to the player.
3. Apply the exact V48 result. Promote `al_literatura_097` (Făt-Frumos), retain
   `al_gastronomie_026`, `al_gastronomie_030`, and `al_viata_de_roman_092` as pending A5
   holds, and remove the other 17 records. Preserve every removed source row and review
   binding in the committed audit; do not fill the shelf by quota.
4. Add no new board, alias, node, edge, graph, or KG content in this gate. Regenerate pack
   rankings and derived metadata while preserving the frozen Intrusul/Perechi boards
   payload. Do not deploy V48 as part of the gate.
5. Preserve deterministic hidden answers and session behavior, including the
   7,200-second session TTL and 1,000-session cap.

This resolves ADR-0065's deferred Alchimie apply. Its authored-wave, derived-freeze, and
other owner-decision clauses remain in force.

## Consequences

Alchimie becomes 82 records = 79 approved/selectable + 3 pending, with high-water mark
106. The original-game pack becomes 618 records = 610 approved + 8 pending and 449
pilot-eligible. The one release addition is a short, bounded Romanian fairy-tale craft;
the three alcohol-dependent boards stay unserved, and 17 weak or recycled boards leave
the dormant queue.

Future Alchimie gates carry a small additional evidence artifact, but reviewers see the
same openings and routes as players instead of inferring playability from global graph
closure. The exact dossiers, source records, projection audit, independent verdicts, and
applied digests remain replay provenance for every removal.

The derived catalog remains 336 boards (183 Intrusul / 153 Perechi), and its frozen
boards-payload SHA-256 remains
`71a2acefb7e0ec62da32ad2645238d73d5e83375808160c0bd1800febd3a73b6`.
