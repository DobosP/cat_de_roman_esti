Author research, 2026-09-15. These notes are proposal provenance, not independent approval.
All descriptions and explanations are original paraphrases. Recipe quantities, photographs
and full source text are not copied. The world presents recipe ideas, not cooking instructions.

- Negresă: [Laura Laurențiu's cocoa version](https://www.lauralaurentiu.ro/retete-culinare/prajituri-torturi/negresa-de-post.html)
  combines flour/cocoa with sugar, water, oil and leavening before baking. Her
  [2017 chocolate version](https://www.lauralaurentiu.ro/retete-culinare/prajituri-torturi/negresa-cu-cheesecake-si-zmeura-reteta-video.html)
  separately identifies the negresă base: melted chocolate/butter, eggs, sugar and flour.
  Only that base is represented; cheesecake and raspberry decoration are not claimed.
  [Robert Eisler's own recipe](https://www.roberteisler.ro/retete/prajitura-negresa-sau-brownie)
  independently corroborates the familiar tray-baked cake identity and squares.
- Cremă de zahăr ars: [Iuliana Sbîrnea](https://bucate-aromate.ro/2023/08/crema-de-zahar-ars/)
  mixes uncooked egg and milk custard, uses caramel to line the vessel, bakes in a water
  bath, chills, then inverts. Both routes preserve that order. Existing cooked pastry
  cream is deliberately not an input. [Gina Bradea's 2017 recipe](https://pofta-buna.com/crema-zahar-ars-reteta-veche/)
  is a recognition lead for independent checking, not a second author verification.
- Budincă de paste: [Laura Laurențiu's 2019 sweet version](https://www.lauralaurentiu.ro/retete-culinare/deserturi-dulciuri-de-casa/budinca-de-taitei-cu-branza-la-cuptor.html)
  uses cooked pasta, cheese, eggs and optional raisins. Search supplied detailed source
  text; a later direct open hit an intermittent site challenge. Alcohol-soaked raisins
  in the source are not reproduced: the proposal explicitly hydrates them in water.
  [Majda's own televised recipe, 4 May 2026](https://www.protv.ro/emisiuni/vorbeste-lumea/articol/126330-majda-a-gatit-macaroane-cu-branza-la-vorbeste-lumea-reteta-rapida-gata-in-mai-putin-de-o-ora-care-cucereste-pe-toata-lumea)
  directly supports savory egg/sour-cream binding and oven setting. The old prepared
  pasta is not treated as raw dough. Only its cooked-pasta-and-cheese form is reused.
- Pavlova: [Laura Laurențiu's 2009 recipe](https://www.lauralaurentiu.ro/retete-culinare/prajituri-torturi/prajitura-pavlova.html)
  assembles a baked, cooled meringue base with whipped cream and fruit. Both routes
  respect the existing cooked Bezea concept; neither converts it back into raw foam.
  No contested origin claim is embedded. [PRO TV's 2022 presentation](https://www.protv.ro/emisiuni/vorbeste-lumea/clip/42035-tort-pavlova-un-desert-simplu-si-delicios)
  supplies an independent national-broadcast recognition signal.
- Profiterol: [Gina Bradea's 2015 first-person recipe](https://pofta-buna.com/profiterol-cu-inghetata-frisca-si-ciocolata/)
  bakes small round choux shells, cools and fills them, and serves with ice cream and a
  chocolate/cream sauce. Her pastry-shop account documents an older Romanian presence.
  The fluid ganache route abstracts that sauce; no premade eclair is reshaped and no raw
  choux is filled. A [Pizza Hut Romania menu](https://prod-phd-cms.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/meniu-phphdphe-rev4-5-200801102025.pdf)
  surfaced a 2025 commercial recognition lead for independent checking.

The five labels have no normalized collision with the current KG's names or aliases.
Every old world concept and recipe record remains exact. All five selected results have
two routes; Bezea and Paste cu brânză gain their first onward use. Baked cake, baked
custard, bound pasta pudding, assembled meringue dessert and filled choux are different
preparations rather than added flavor variants of an existing label.
