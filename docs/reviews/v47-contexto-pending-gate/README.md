# V47 Cald sau Rece pending-target quality gate

_Reviewed and closed 2026-08-01. This report records the evidence for ADR-0071._

## Scope and release bar

V47 rebuilt exact dossiers for all 13 pending Cald sau Rece records against the V46 pack,
current KG, current rubric, the closed 71-node feedback-proxy set, and all 453 projected
guess terms. The exact sorted ID set has SHA-256
`508ac4014f6c519fc0891b92474e683e9a26078e86091cee8ee77a1aa10e97c1`.

The quota-free release bar required C1–C6, broad Romanian recognition, a unique and
dominant target sense, at least five recognizable direct predecessors, an intuitive warm
opener, and a useful rather than merely large feedback field. Two independent promotions
were required to ship; either rejection rejected; A5 owner-boundary cases stayed pending.
V42 bindings were stale and V44's exact gate covered two different, already-approved
records, so neither supplied a V47 judgment.

## Deterministic and vocabulary census

All 13 targets are unique within the pending batch and none repeats another current
Contexto target. Every target is reachable from all 2,288 raw graph nodes and clears the
engine's responsive floor. The deterministic critique found no failure and one warning:
Sonicitate has salience 0.152 below the greu review floor of 0.20. Those broad floors are
necessary but do not prove that nearby words feel right.

The lexical pass added nothing by quota. Existing morphology is adequate for Mâncare,
and speculative exact aliases were either broader than the target or attached to rejected
records. In particular, `ploaia` cannot safely solve the weather target because that
surface already resolves to the served Cargo song node. `hrană` and `alimente` may be
considered only in a separately bound non-winning projection review; neither is an exact
same-sense alias.

## Applied outcome

- `ct_gastronomie_300` becomes the one new common-word target.
- Ten failed dormant records are removed; their exact dossiers, bindings, record digests,
  and two-reviewer artifact remain committed as archive provenance.
- `ct_meme_net_238` and `ct_societate_257` remain pending and unserved for explicit owner
  disposition; no pending record is promoted to fill a shelf.
- Cald sau Rece becomes 207 records = 205 approved + 2 pending, with 202 unique selectable
  targets. The original pack becomes 635 records = 609 approved + 26 pending, with 448
  runtime-eligible records.
- The KG, graph, alias/projection inventories, scoring, hidden-answer boundary, and session
  behavior are unchanged. Rankings and derived metadata are regenerated while the frozen
  336-board Intrusul/Perechi payload stays byte-identical.

Machine evidence is `critique.json`, the 13 files under `dossiers/`, and
`contexto_verdicts.json`. Contexto has no generic rejection ledger, so ADR-0071 and the
V47 regression contract preserve this exact archive and require a fresh bound repair
review before any rejected target returns.
