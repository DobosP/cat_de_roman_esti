# V67 online-content-and-social-media-morphology funnel

This directory records the fixed 50-surface, two-reviewer funnel governed by ADR-0091.
It considered two genitive/dative forms for each of 25 existing online-content and
social-media concepts. Forty-eight sense-qualified forms passed both reviews.
`fluxului` and `fluxurilor` were rejected because _flux_ ordinarily denotes a tide or
surge, a physical, technological, or information flow, and the continuously updated
feed of posts and clips on a social platform. No quota applied.

Qualifiers such as `comentariilor publice de pe internet`, `algoritmilor platformelor
sociale`, `audiențelor românești online`, and `capturilor de ecran salvate` bind the
accepted surfaces to one existing online-content sense. The accepted forms add no
concept or sense. They remain exact aliases consumed only by typed Contexto input and
otherwise-legal Lanț hops. The wave preserves projections, graph topology, game
records, ranking rows, the frozen derived payload, session bounds, accounts, frontend,
privacy, and deployment state.
