# Author source checks — 2026-09-17

These are unapproved drafting notes, not independent judgments. No source article, image or video is copied into the repository.

**Supă de roșii.** [JamilaCuisine's 2015 original recipe](https://jamilacuisine.ro/supa-de-rosii-cu-orez-reteta-video/) explicitly lists tomatoes, rice, onion, garlic and a homemade chicken-soup base. Its author describes a childhood family preparation. This supports the recognizable soup/base pairing and broad Romanian familiarity. The ingredient list and explanatory article were read; the linked video was not separately watched. [Dana Burlacu's original 2020 recipe](https://prajituricisialtele.ro/2020/08/10/supa-de-rosii-cu-orez/) directly describes boiling tomatoes with water, adding sautéed vegetables, blending and adding separately cooked rice. The draft distinguishes those two approaches without promising that every tomato soup contains rice or meat stock.

**Mâncare de spanac.** [Laura Sava's 2010 original recipe](https://www.laurasava.ro/spanac-cu-lapte/) names spinach, garlic, flour and milk, boils/drains/chops the spinach, and combines it with a cooked white sauce. The author records the dish as a childhood family meal. This directly supports both proposed ingredient cues and the explicitly milk-based definition. [Rețete Fel de Fel's original 2017 preparation](https://retetefeldefel.ro/spanac-cu-smantana-si-usturoi/) independently demonstrates the familiar spinach/garlic dish, but uses cooking cream. It supplies recognition/context only, not authority to reinterpret this world's fermented Smântână as sweet cooking cream.
