# Intrusul / Perechi editorial expansion notes

Valid until: this V92 candidate is superseded; historical authoring evidence only.

The frozen V38 catalog was generated from 123 historical Conexiuni boards. The
current generator intentionally reproduces 183 Intrusul and 153 Perechi records,
so merely refreshing source digests does not add content. Expanding the old source
snapshot could also substitute retained variants/ranks. The proposed additive
catalog retains every old board and authors new, separately reviewed records.

25 Intrusul predicates use 95 distinct visible concepts, 92 unseen in old Intrusul.
20 Perechi boards use 154 distinct visible concepts, 130 unseen in old Perechi.
Their 80 pairs are unique across this wave and 73 are unseen in old Perechi.
No new board shares more than two concepts with any old board; no two new boards
of the same game share more than one concept. Counts refer to game exposure, not
to new graph identities: the shared KG is unchanged.

All Intrusul trios are new and each has at least two strong (.60) links, a shared
node type, and no non-distractor edge to its intruder. All four Perechi intended
pairs have a strong link and all 24 cross-pairs remain below .60. These numeric
checks are necessary but cannot prove natural-language uniqueness.

Authoring exclusions include otherwise obvious but sparsely linked groups: writing
instruments, cutlery, named ciorbe, three Creangă tales, castles, rivers and mountain
massifs. No graph link was fabricated solely to make a board pass. Existing tennis
trio was omitted as a repeat. Unused Conexiuni-derived variants were not wholesale
accepted: exclusions include a cherry outside a fasting-food group, biology outside
forest animals, alcohol predicates, and multiple identical trios with new intruders.

Manual Perechi composition avoids two author/work pairs that permit a writers-versus-
works alternate partition; actor/character mirror pairs are separated too. Siblings
and spouses are placed on different boards to avoid male-versus-female alternatives.
George Enescu's Rapsodia Română and Porumbescu's Balada pentru vioară are separated,
since Porumbescu also composed a Rapsodia română. Cultural associations share the
broad everyday-life shelf with familiar objects; no niche topic category is promised.

Factual feedback already changed a workshop ruler intruder to a toothbrush: a ruler
is a valid workshop tool. The ambiguous garden-vegetable/pumpkin-family example was
excluded. Nuts use a culinary shell/kernel predicate rather than a botanical claim.
The independent factual and root quality reviews still decide all candidate verdicts.
