Alchimie proposes two familiar preparations and five recipes:

- **Ostropel** through Carne de pui + Usturoi or Carne de pui + Sos de roșii.
  [Gina Bradea's original recipe](https://pofta-buna.com/ostropel-de-pui-reteta-clasica/)
  directly documents the chicken, tomato and garlic preparation. The description says
  this world uses the tomato version; it does not erase white ostropel variants.
  Both explanations explicitly cook the chicken. Additional ingredients and cooking remain
  explicit abstractions, as in the existing recipe-idea game.
- **Salată de fructe** through Fruct + Cuțit or Fruct + Frișcă.
  [Laura Adamache's 2015 recipe](https://www.lauraadamache.ro/2015/04/salata-de-fructe.html)
  cuts and mixes different fruits and serves the chilled mixture with optional whipped cream.
  The first route means preparing several fruits, not turning a single cut piece into a
  full salad. No alcoholic dressing or nutritional claim is included.
- **Pâine + Chiftele de legume → Sandviș** makes an existing terminal word useful again.
  [Daniela Niculi's original vegetable-patty sandwich](https://danielaniculi.ro/2020/03/19/sandvis-vegan-sau-cum-sa-salvezi-prea-multa-quinoa-pusa-la-fiert/)
  supplies a directly relevant recipe lead; the search index gave recipe content but a
  direct open showed a challenge page. [Gourmandelle's own recipe](https://gourmandelle.com/ro/chiftle-prajite-cu-ciuperci/)
  explicitly serves cooked vegetarian patties between bread with hummus and vegetables.
  The generalization to the existing vegetable-patty concept needs independent factual
  review; no raw batter or returned-to-raw ingredient is implied.

Intrusul proposes Furtună / Fulger / Tunet versus Lună under **Fenomene produse în atmosferă**.
The [National Weather Service's thunder explanation](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-science-thunder)
and [lightning glossary](https://forecast.weather.gov/glossary.php?word=lightning)
support the atmospheric phenomena; [NASA identifies the Moon as Earth's satellite](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/).
The wording does not claim every storm has thunder, and does not group a storm with
things that merely accompany it. All three native internal strengths are 0.9, every
intruder edge is zero, and the existing rating gate permits starter selection.

Perechi proposes Frunză–Ramură, Câine–Pisică, Banană–Fruct and Grâu–Făină.
Original dictionary entries support [branch](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/ramur%C4%83),
[leaf](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/frunz%C4%83), [cat](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/pisic%C4%83),
[banana](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/banan%C4%83) and [flour](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/f%C4%83in%C4%83)
meanings. The pair labels distinguish attachment, familiar pets, food classification
and milling. They do not claim all leaves grow on branches, bananas grow on trees or all
flour comes from wheat. Native pair strengths are 0.99/0.9/0.86/0.8, with zero cross-pair
strength and exactly one native perfect matching. This mechanical result is not a
substitute for an independent human-semantic partition critique.
