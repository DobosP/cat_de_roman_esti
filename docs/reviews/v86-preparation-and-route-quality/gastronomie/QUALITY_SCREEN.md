## Real endpoints and corrections

Initial private probes made 153 first guesses across the five targets. They exposed
an important reverse-association defect, so it was corrected before staging:

| Pair | Before | After exact pair correction |
|---|---|---|
| Ecler→Cremă de vanilie | rank 322, distance 4, cold | rank 2, distance 1, hot; still Ecler and nonwinning |
| Savarină→Frișcă | rank 140, distance 4, lukewarm | rank 2, distance 1, hot; still Savarină and nonwinning |

Only these two target scopes were re-probed afterward: 54 first guesses. The
independent acceptance and exact source binding are in the parent review directory.
All before evidence remains alongside the after evidence. The correction adds no
graph direction or synonym and does not make either pastry win a cream target.

Five private clue→repeat→resume→exact-win journeys passed initially, and the two
corrected targets passed those journeys again. Clues remain Mâncare, Pahar,
Prăjitură, Frigider and Lapte, each rank 2. Both Lanț branches win in two moves and
resume correctly; entering Înghețată directly at the start is rejected without a move.
Both route choices appear in the bounded menu alongside unrelated local options.
These checks bypass pending/public selection intentionally; final public journeys
and exact dossier bindings remain required after staging.

Remaining realism debt is explicit: Sarmale is warm for cheese/milk and rank 30/warm
for both cream targets; Stilou is lukewarm for milk/vanilla cream; butter and several
secondary dessert associations remain weak. The corrected defining cores are still
substantially clearer than those indirect routes. Biscuit was deferred because its
five-link count concealed weak flour/dessert feedback; no seventh ref was fabricated.
