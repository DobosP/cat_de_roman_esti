All four leave attempts at zero. `fier` offers `fluier`, and `reparație` offers `pârâu`, under “Ai vrut să scrii”. For Penar seed 5, `rechizite`, `rechizită` and `rechizite școlare` are also absent and free. Proposed outcome: clearly identify vocabulary coverage rather than a wrong answer, state that no attempt was used, and avoid presenting weak spelling resemblance as understood intent. A possible short message is “Cuvântul nu este încă în vocabularul jocului. Nu ai pierdut nicio încercare.” Preserve the typed input and normal next guess. Distinguish an advisory spelling suggestion from a confirmed correction; do not submit one automatically. Acceptance: these observed inputs remain free, normal words continue to rank, suggestions never reveal the target or its private scoring proxy, and ordinary real misspellings still receive justified help. This is the highest priority because it affects an ordinary player's first guesses, without requiring a graph change.

Respect a player who moved focus elsewhere while waiting; do not replay the mutation or recreate the V95 stale cached-focus reference. Acceptance: committed/uncommitted failures, deliberate focus movement, depleted or filtered ingredients, 320px/doubled text, cached repeats and changed saved-collection ownership.

Preserve bidirectional truth and variant language; no new edge, alias, easier route or advance route disclosure. Acceptance: every approved phrase appears in actual GET/rendered earned paths in both traversal directions, remains readable on a narrow screen, and falls back safely when its edge changes.

## Input coverage boundary

`input-probe.json` checks the existing exact resolver, projections, advisory/fuzzy results and all stored labels/aliases. `unealtă/unelte`, `sculă/scule`, `fier`, and the three tested rechizite forms have neither an exact identity nor a reviewed projection. There is no hidden “Rechizite școlare” record to expose. Substring matches for fier are unrelated identities such as Porțile de Fier, Las Fierbinți, cooking forms and Fierăstrău; they do not license aliases for the metal.

Existing usable possibilities are `metal`, `Clește`/`clești`, `Penar`, `Creion`, `Ghiozdan` and `Fierăstrău`. `a repara` and `atelier` are existing reviewed projection terms, not direct KG identities. The noun `reparație` must not be silently aliased to either action, a particular tool or a river. Broad category coverage belongs in an independently governed vocabulary proposal, separate from the truthful UI fix. No new graph vocabulary was counted in this critique.

Alchimie expansion also has a practical planning limit: 247/256 concepts and seven older recipe-book generations are already present. Prefer useful alternate routes and onward uses over a large batch of new terminal labels; the next compatibility generation reaches the current eight-generation bound.
