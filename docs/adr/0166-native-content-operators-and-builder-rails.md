# ADR-0166 — Native content operators and builder rails

Date: 2026-10-05
Status: accepted implementation; full fresh-checkout qualification pending
Amends: ADR-0165's remaining content-operator boundary and ADR-0102's operator commands.

## Decision

The owner requested complete native content tooling. `cat-content-ops` ports pack-only
candidate import, critique/dossiers, portable independent artifact assembly/application,
live Alchimie projection audits, ranking, frozen derived catalog, inventory delta, and
JSONL submission list/stage/promote/reject. `cat-content-rail` ports quick, world, recipe
extension and reserve build/audit paths from a private authored JSON input with source
provenance. The bootstrap extracted authored constants from original modules; generated
serving fixtures and the private bundle are not authored input. All four baseline builds
reproduce their original bytes and pins; data, review histories and rejection debt remain.

Native dossiers bind `review_source_version=native-contentops-v1`, exact records, KG,
rubric, generator/runtime/policy inputs and ledgers. Preserve original Python-bound V2
history; obtain fresh exact-ID analyst/verifier judgments against native dossiers. Raw
quality keep still stages pending. Alchimie reviews independently bind the same exact
live sparse projection audit, routes, par and choice bounds. Artifact assembly does not
promote. Two independent unanimous approvals plus strict prospective validation are
required; rejections and persistent high-water marks prevent retired-ID reuse.

Mutations require explicit write flags. All native writers share the operator lock,
immutable byte/presence/mode readsets, under-lock and postwrite runtime membership checks,
atomic synced sibling replacement, deep postvalidation and verified rollback. Symlink,
stale source, malformed schema/Unicode, partial write and absent-target failures refuse.
Submission list output omits author identity. Queue entries must stage into the same
independent review rail; queue-only rejection cannot discard staged content or its debt.

Builder candidates never grant approval. Quick/world/extensions/reserve proposal and
installation gates bind source version/parent hash, exact candidate, independent factual
and quality reviews, native live audit, final independent judgments and expected artifact
SHA. World transitions preserve the current and earlier books and every earned prefix.
A signed staged artifact passes its rail's deep validation while other authorities remain
pinned. Default export/startup still refuses its changed bytes until the reviewed authority
pin/code update and rebuild. This separates a valid staging transition from publication.

## Qualification and retention

Native operator race/vet tests match all 716 ranking rows/metadata, the 336 frozen derived
boards and 709 approved dossiers, and exercise synthetic positive/refusal/rollback cases.
Builder audits cover 85 quick boards, 33 goal modes, all 351 recipes, nine archived books
and 1009 prefixes. Synthetic version-two installation and sealed-export refusal pass.
Independent review findings were fixed and rechecked. No real imports or promotions ran.

Complete fresh-checkout Go/Node/browser/disposable-PostgreSQL qualification remains the
release gate before required legacy retirement. Keep every Python/Rust source, test,
reference and rollback profile; their retained jobs must not silently certify native
success. Production remains anonymous and accounts/proposals off.
