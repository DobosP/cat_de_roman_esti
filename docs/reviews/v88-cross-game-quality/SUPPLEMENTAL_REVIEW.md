# Independent V88 topology and feedback-cost review

Valid until: the bound runtime, artifacts, tests or decisions change — then treat as history.

Accept this local iteration with the explicit limits below. I found no additional
material blocker requiring reversal of the reviewed graph or content. This is
conditional on the required revised tests and full integration passing; those checks
were in flight when I reviewed the frozen test changes.

ADR-0131 now records the actual design change: Făraș→Mătură→Podea opens the former
V31 sink for exactly Burete de vase, Detergent, Aspirator, Făraș, Mop and Găleată.
The relations are true; earlier blanket isolation is no longer the current decision.
The original ADR bodies remain historical, with narrow supersession pointers.

The revised tests are appropriate. They retain the old cut on reconstructed V87,
require exactly those six current escape sources, verify both directed bridge roles,
and remove only Făraș→Mătură in an in-memory graph to prove isolation is restored.
V44 protects all other legacy proxy sources and pins all 71 scorer mappings. I also
independently compared those mappings to baseline: they are exact. This is a bounded
topology amendment, not permission to drop isolation, privacy or scoring assertions.

The Alchimie test update preserves the exact historical 79-board digest and median
0.66 on V87. Current stock must equal that history plus the reviewed Cremșnit round;
its extra three routes raise the median to 0.67. Existing performance and recipe
bounds remain. A legitimate stock change is not hidden by deleting its old evidence.

The final impact evidence binds the current nine captured sources. I verified both
complete comparison capture archives and decoded hashes, plus every derived report
hash. All 655 old source records, 82 Alchimie profiles, 100 Lanț profiles and 336
derived board rows remain exact; old eligibility is preserved. One nonshortest
Lanț menu entry changes, while all 206 shown shortest first hops remain available.
These facts constrain gameplay impact; they do not prove every rank improved.

There are real semantic costs. Correcting fermented cream makes these guesses toward
Frișcă worse: Tort Diplomat becomes 345/cold, Cremșnit 504/cold, Pișcot 944/very cold
and Prăjitură 181/lukewarm. No reviewer should label these as improvements. The
existing unrelated Zacuscă cue still scores 24/warm, exposing remaining graph-ranking
noise. However, qualified whipping cream is 2/hot and the captured familiar Zahăr
guess is 11/hot; the target keeps accessible approaches and remains eligible. Keeping
the false sour-cream relation would conceal the underlying ranking problem. A future
focused dessert-cue review should address it without undoing the corrected ownership.

Retiring Casa/Aspirator approximations accounts for 290 of 306 cooler observations.
Native ownership removes broad accidental affinities but also loses some plausible
home context: Taburet→Viața la bloc becomes 678/cold and the drill-noise neighbor
becomes 1096/very cold. Those costs are acceptable here because no input or old
playable stock is lost and the new native words have specific reviewed uses, but they
remain quality work. Restoring the universal proxies merely to regain rank numbers
would be unconvincing. Likewise, incidental warmer paths are not automatically gains.

This review read existing impact evidence and frozen test code; it did not rerun the
11,233-observation matrix or either full backend suite. The reports sample 47 inputs,
not all possible guesses or Romanian human play. The decision is readiness for local
V88 landing after green integration, not deployment or a claim of release-ready
semantic quality. Exact bindings and follow-up priorities are in `supplemental-review.json`.
