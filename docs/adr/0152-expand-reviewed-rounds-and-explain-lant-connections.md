# ADR-0152: Expand reviewed rounds and explain Lanț connections

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-15
- Extends [ADR-0151](0151-expand-game-vocabulary-and-search-earned-recipes.md).

## Decision

After the owner requested landing V93 and starting V94, develop this bounded version
from `c971846` in `feat/v94-words-and-clearer-connections`. Preserve the review gates,
existing gameplay records, session bounds, privacy and saved-collection compatibility.

Add twelve reviewed rounds/targets: one Conexiuni board, two Cald sau Rece targets,
one Lanț round, four Intrusul boards and four Perechi boards. Raw and allocated-ID
reviews are separate; only four of the five proposed pack records are promoted.
The Sfinx→Crucea Caraiman proposal is rejected because its existing target description
is visible to players and conflicts with the monument owner's location account.
Its identifier remains reserved and the rejection is recorded in the durable ledger.
Sound connection captions do not establish that an entire round is factually sound.

Expand Alchimie by seven preparations and seventeen recipes to 242 concepts and 332
recipes. Alternate approaches and useful onward recipes extend discovery without
changing any of the 235 previous concept records or 315 recipes. Six earlier saved
books retain their earned progress. Keep the starter/supply/goal structures and all
existing capacity and provenance rules. Complete independent semantic reviews and
final artifact/runtime audits gate installation of both the world and quick catalogs.

Add 21 exact-edge-bound Lanț captions: thirteen across the V93 routes and eight for
food and Bucegi connections. Every phrase receives separate factual and quality
acceptance and remains truthful in reverse traversal. Existing eighty captions,
graph topology, edge metadata and scoring stay exact. The Bucegi phrase identifies
natural rocks and a monument in the massif; it makes no unsupported plateau-location
claim. Captions for existing geographic edges remain separate from the rejected new
round. Changed edge snapshots retain the neutral fallback behavior.

Let earned Lanț steps and their relationship text wrap within the screen width.
At 320px with doubled text, the previous hidden-scrollbar strip prevented reading
whole captions together. The correction is scoped to Lanț and retains focus,
keyboard controls, actions, recovery and state ownership. No extra player action is
required to read a complete earned step.

## Evidence and limits

[The V94 archive](../reviews/v94-words-and-clearer-connections/README.md) preserves
raw proposals, exclusions, exact independent reviews, BFF journeys and before/after
captures. The inaccurate Caraiman description was caught during final verification;
initial acceptance artifacts and the correction remain explicit historical evidence.

Strict inverses restore the five original core artifacts and the original 104-entry
Lanț rejection ledger before earlier historical assertions. Current tests separately
verify the new 105th rejection, reserved ID and blocked reimport. Current Contexto
profile and seeded browser snapshots follow the expanded approved inventory while
retaining exact before/after evidence.

Integrated gate results and inventory belong in [STATUS](../STATUS.md). Automated
and agent reviews do not establish human enjoyment or physical-device acceptance.
V93 publication is authorized; this V94 session prepares a local candidate. Production
deployment and another automatic version are outside this request.
