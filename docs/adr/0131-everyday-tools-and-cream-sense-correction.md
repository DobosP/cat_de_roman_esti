# ADR-0131: Add usable everyday tools and separate whipping cream from sour cream

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-08
- Partially supersedes: ADR-0123 (fermented-Smântână→Frișcă edge), ADR-0039
  (six cleaning-node sink cut and deferred Mătură ownership), and ADR-0068
  (that topology premise only).

## Context

V88's household boards need concrete geometric, cleaning, seating and cooking tools.
Independent recipe review also found that the proposed Diplomat seed denotes fermented
smântână, while its quoted whipping-cream source uses sweet cream. The existing de8676
edge inherited this unsupported sense substitution. Structural solvability did not make
that recipe honest.

## Decision

Add seven independently reviewed concepts: Compas, Echer, Raportor, Mătură, Taburet,
Cratiță and qualified Smântână dulce pentru frișcă. Add 28 accepted forms: 25 ordinary
inflections and three qualified cream expressions, with no new lexical equivalent.
Keep bare smântână and its existing fermented owner/forms intact. New cream expressions
must explicitly identify the whipping use; do not import bare smântână dulce as a
universal whipping-cream alias.

Add 33 source-backed directed links and remove exactly de8676, for a net32. The new
cream's actual ingredient relation to Tort Diplomat belongs in the graph even though it
makes the original proposed seed set a one-pair answer. Do not withhold a true defining
edge to keep a desired difficulty. Diplomat remains held after the screened alternatives
proved shallow, unintuitive or dependent on unused seeds. The remaining Cremșnit and
two Conexiuni candidates still require their separate bound promotion judgments.

Canonicalize edge identity exactly as the supported preflight does. Every new node must
meet its four-distinct-neighbor/two-same-category floor through specific sourced uses.
Do not pad edges or relax those floors. Original graph proposals failed these checks;
retain that evidence and the superseded initial independent judgment.

Retire Mătură's Aspirator projection and Taburet's Casa projection now that their native
owners exist. The other465 projection rows and all26 domains remain. Keep existing
numeric rules,71 legacy proxies and four prior native-audit tuples intact. Replace only
the cleaning audit representative with retained Pămătuf on the same Aspirator anchor;
this metadata substitution cannot influence live scoring. Other25 audit examples stay exact.

The reviewed Făraș→Mătură→Podea path opens the former V31 cleaning sink for exactly
Burete de vase, Detergent, Aspirator, Făraș, Mop and Găleată. Their real cleaning
associations now reach the mature graph. This is a deliberate bounded topology change,
not a reason to weaken isolation assertions or pretend the old design remains current.
Keep V31's original excluded Mătură ownership testable in historical scope; V88 now
accepts that reviewed tool. Keep the original boundary testable on reconstructed V87
and require exactly this six-node
opening on current data. Other V30–V33 meshes retain their previous sink behavior; ADR-0038's
V30 decision is unchanged. All71 Contexto scorer proxies remain closed and exact despite
the new raw reachability. The measured82 old Alchimie and100 Lanț profiles remain exact;
this is not a promise that every old-to-old graph distance is unchanged.

## Acceptance and limits

The final module and candidate bytes have independent indexed node/form/edge/removal
review, plus the actual canonical preflight. Preserve every other old edge, owner, form
and legacy puzzle; measure old recipe books, route profiles, feedback and selectable pools.
Use supported graph/import/generation transactions, strict critique, exact Alchimie
projection evidence, public journeys and the full V88 integration before landing.

Compas/Raportor secondary senses remain disclosed, as does Mătură's accent-normalized
verb homograph. No hidden Contexto targets for the new nodes are approved here. The
derived source set remains frozen; a metadata refresh is not new Intrusul/Perechi content.
Source evidence and failed/final proposals live under
`docs/reviews/v88-cross-game-quality/author-screening/` and its final graph review.
