## Identity and ordinary preparation

Romanian dictionary evidence defines a clătită as a thin preparation cooked in fat and filled with dulceață, marmalade, cheese, or vegetables. The definition appears in DLRLC (1955–1957) and the same food sense is retained in later DEX material.

Current Radio România Internațional coverage gives the ordinary batter as flour, egg, and milk, cooked as a thin layer in a pan. It describes sweet versions with dulceață or chocolate and savory versions with mushrooms, onion, or meat. This supports the KG description, the directed Făină and Ou ingredient links, and the sweet/savory neighborhood.

The nine incoming non-distractor associations were inspected: Făină, Ou, Dulceață, Desert, Papanași, Gogoși, Plăcinte, Înghețată, and Brânză cu smântână. None is factually contradictory. Făină and Ou are ordinary batter ingredients; Dulceață is a standard filling; sweet clătite are desserts related to other home pastries; savory/dairy fillings and serving with ice cream are ordinary variants. These facts establish semantic plausibility, while their rank warmth remains a separate gameplay question.

## Recognition and dominant sense

Clătite pass the basic recognition check with independent signals. A national-radio article reports a Romanian festival in its 23rd edition, about 15,000 visitors and roughly 20,000 clătite in the prior edition, and states that the dish is found throughout Romania and in restaurant/pension menus. Current PRO TV programming also uses the unqualified word in culinary headlines. Older dictionary evidence and a 2014 Romanian school worksheet headed “CLĂTITE CU GEM” show that this is not a recent single-media spike.

`Clătite` is morphologically ambiguous: it can also be the feminine plural form of the adjective/participle `clătit`, as in rinsed clothes or dishes. That sense normally modifies an expressed or contextually supplied noun. Used alone as a capitalized plural label for a concrete thing, `Clătite` overwhelmingly cues the lexicalized food noun. The ambiguity is real but does not create a competitive standalone target sense, so no factual or C5 blocker is identified.
