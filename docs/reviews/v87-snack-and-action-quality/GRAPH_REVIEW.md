## Holds and limits

**Keep Pandișpan and Brioșă on hold as hidden targets.** [The Chec/Pandișpan recipe](https://bucate-aromate.ro/2019/07/chec-cu-visine-si-cacao/) explicitly demonstrates overlapping naming and a chec without baking powder. A rigid chec-versus-sponge leavener distinction would therefore be false. Historical yeast brioche and modern quick-batter brioșă also share the Romanian name; accepting their qualified variant edges does not resolve hidden-target ambiguity.

The solid chocolate owner remains distinct from Ciocolată caldă. Bicarbonate, baking powder, gelatin and yeast are not interchangeable ingredients or synonyms. Input-only ingredient nodes do not automatically qualify as secret targets. Runtime feedback, reachable routes, existing projections, selections and actual promotion candidates still require the root’s post-apply measurements and separate bound judgments.
