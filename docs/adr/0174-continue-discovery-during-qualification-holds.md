# ADR-0174: Continue discovery during qualification holds

Valid until: the owner changes the continuous discovery request — then treat as history.

Date: 2026-10-06
Status: partially superseded by ADR-0175 for the stopped Windows loop and Linux source handoff

## Decision

Continue finite, useful candidate discovery while refinement and native qualification wait
for their own admission. A completed or failed source batch chooses a materially different
unexplored frontier; zero eligible additions and an execution hold do not end discovery.
The owner explicitly corrected the former source-idle continuation. This clarifies
[ADR-0173](0173-resume-bounded-content-version-loop.md) and follows the two-track separation
in [ADR-0156](0156-two-track-content-growth.md).

## Context

V1.2 qualified narrow input synonyms, V1.3 specific everyday object functions and V1.4
honest directional links. V99 demonstrated a player-facing caption repair alongside an
uninstalled discovery pool. V1.5 i01 and i02 remain preserved source evidence. Their
adoption holds do not exhaust lexical, everyday-concept, relation, board or repair searches.

## Consequences

Record stable lead IDs, exact source/baseline bindings, ownership/rejection collisions,
qualified facts, competing play and next actions. Use bounded diverse slates without
padding quotas. Preserve rejected/held evidence; reopen it only on material new evidence.
Keep the discovery frontier and qualification queue separate. Source checkpoints and
experimental iteration numbers do not increment an accepted release version.

Use an isolated task branch/worktree, preserve earlier exact receipts and shared main,
and obey current source headroom/budget admission. Native generation, simulations,
builds, runtime/history checks and heavy campaigns remain held until actual owner release
and specifically admitted qualification. Source facts are not numeric weight, gameplay
or enjoyment approval. All applicable graph/target/source/history/privacy/session/editorial
and independent factual/quality/assembled gates remain. The coordinator alone accepts
and lands a fully qualified local version. No push, deployment or deletion is authorized.
