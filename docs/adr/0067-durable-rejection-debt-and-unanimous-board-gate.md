# ADR-0067: Durable rejection debt and unanimous board gate

Date: 2026-07-30
Status: accepted

## Decision

Supersede ADR-0023 with a release gate that compares every Conexiuni candidate against
the full approved, pending, demoted, and rejected inventory; retains rejected boards as
digest-bound tombstones; and requires unanimous deterministic, exact-byte factual,
independent play-quality, and imported real-ID analyst/verifier approval. Direct reuse of
any ADR-0066 demoted quad is forbidden; a derived idea must replace at least two members
and sit beside three fresh groups. Keep the 75 owner-demoted records approved but
unselectable through the mirrored sidecar. Derived-catalog integrity failures return a
controlled 503, and every source change must regenerate rankings and source bindings
while preserving the frozen 336-board Intrusul/Perechi payload.

## Context / why

Replaying the Fable 5 workflow across all 67 original demotions and 268 groups showed
that runtime-only novelty checks hid pending and reserve debt: 211 groups collided
exactly or at three of four in the full pack. The first 66 replacement proposals yielded
44 early survivors, but exact re-review retained only three; later strict rebuilding
retained only two of 11 final raw proposals before their real-ID gate. Automation also
missed true semantic crossfits, type worksheets, obscurity-based difficulty, elastic
association labels, and eight unsafe approved boards (six in the stock sweep and two in
the final incumbent re-audit). Removing rejects without preserving their groups allowed
the same weak idea to return under a new ID. Finally, pack edits made the digest-bound
derived catalog fail during game creation until regeneration.

Why not keep ADR-0023 unchanged: its two layers were necessary but did not bind every
human judgment to exact bytes and complete coverage, did not treat rejected material as
future novelty debt, and focused too much on runtime-eligible survivors. Why not force
replacement quotas: hidden empty shelves are safer than release content whose difficulty
comes from obscurity or whose partition is arguable.

## Consequences

Candidate batches may legitimately import nothing. Rejections grow a reviewed tombstone
fixture and therefore make future authoring stricter; direct, three-of-four, and
eight-of-sixteen reskins fail before promotion. The deterministic parser now covers
literal surface rules, type shortcuts, mirrors, label leakage, vague predicates, and
projected member overuse, while human reviewers remain responsible for facts,
recognition, clue arc, and fun. Eight additional approved boards are excluded through the
owner sidecar without deleting historical records. Any pack, KG, rubric, or ranking
change leaves derived games unavailable until both catalog copies and the pinned digest
are regenerated and verified. The frozen derived `boards` array, session TTL/caps,
hidden-answer boundary, scores, and API operation IDs do not change.
