## Independently checked facts

[DEX 2009's original entry, reproduced by dexonline](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/praf/876302),
distinguishes ordinary fine particulate matter from the separately qualified powder
meanings. A household dust cue therefore need not imply that every powder is soil,
or that Praf becomes a native synonym of a cleaning implement.

The [Leifheit 41410 dustpan-and-brush description](https://leifheit.ro/ro/acasa/164-leifheit-set-maturafaras.html)
expressly collects fine dust and pairs the brush with the collecting scoop.
The older [CADE Făraș definition](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/f%C4%83ra%C8%99/1341866)
supplies the same collecting-tool sense. The target's existing description promises
exactly that purpose, making frozen Praf feedback a specific player-facing defect.

[Kärcher's household vacuum page](https://www.karcher.com/ro/ro/home-and-garden/produse/aspiratoare-c4250)
explicitly describes removing deposited household dust from floors and other surfaces.
It supports the current floor-appliance Aspirator owner. Industrial extraction and
nasal aspirators do not defeat the dominant household sense in this themed round.
No universal water requirement or wet-vacuum capability is inferred.

For Mop I checked more than a generic cleaning claim. The original
[Vileda Turbo 2in1 replacement-head description](https://www.viledaromania.ro/Mops/Cap-de-mop-Turbo-2in1/p/151609)
expressly describes microfibres collecting dust and hair from floors. Its ordinary
rotating wet-mop context fits the current absorbent floor-washing owner; it is not
necessary to reinterpret Mop as a separate specialist dry-duster object.
[Vileda's spray mop](https://www.viledaromania.ro/spray-mops/1-2-spray-max)
provides corroborating particle collection in a different ordinary mop design.
The [DEX/DOOM entry](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/mop) documents the established
household word. Neither product's efficiency percentages are accepted as game facts.

These sources establish concrete associations. Choosing hot, nonwinning feedback
under the existing bounded policy is editorial judgment; they do not prove rank 2
