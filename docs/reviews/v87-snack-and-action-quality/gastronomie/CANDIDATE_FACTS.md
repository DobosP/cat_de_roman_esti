## Concrete food identities and original evidence

| Ref | Target | Factual core |
|---|---|---|
| contexto[0] | Biscuit | Baked snack; directly demonstrated flour, butter, sugar, leavener and oven cues |
| contexto[1] | Chec | Familiar cake family; actual cocoa/oil/powder variant and milk-serving association |
| contexto[2] | Cremșnit | Baked sheets and vanilla cream; starch, dusting and chilling; regional name cremeș |
| contexto[3] | Tort Diplomat | Assembled chilled dessert using pișcoturi, cream, gelatin and fruit |
| contexto[4] | Pișcot | Elongated porous baked pastry, also used in layered desserts |
| contexto[5] | Ciocolată | Existing solid-food identity, with two additional documented ingredient variants |

- **Biscuit:** the [original manufacturer recipe](https://www.oetker.ro/retete/r/biscuiti-cu-unt-love-letters)
  explicitly lists flour, butter, sugar, baking powder and food bicarbonate, then
  cuts and bakes the pieces. Its broader recipe catalogue includes sweet and savory
  biscuits, so sugar/butter/leavener edges are variants, not universal predicates.
  Existing tea, oats and nut associations remain separate. This is an ordinary food
  name; the fact that Pișcot belongs to the same broad food vocabulary does not make
  the two exact identities synonymous.
- **Chec:** [JamilaCuisine's original2013 recipe](https://jamilacuisine.ro/chec-pufos-cu-cacao-reteta-video/)
  demonstrates the six ingredient cues and explicitly describes serving slices with
  milk. [The independent2019 recipe](https://bucate-aromate.ro/2019/07/chec-cu-visine-si-cacao/)
  calls its chec a pandișpan and does not use baking powder. These sources establish
  familiar sustained use, while refuting an artificial absolute leavener boundary.
  Pandișpan remains held as another hidden target; its native word is still useful.
- **Cremșnit:** [the original2014 recipe](https://jamilacuisine.ro/prajitura-cremsnit-pas-cu-pas-reteta-video/)
  uses sheets, vanilla cream and starch. [A second original preparation](https://bucate-aromate.ro/2025/10/cremsnit-cu-aluat-de-casa/)
  explicitly chills the assembled pastry and dusts it with powdered sugar.
  [The original2024 article](https://jamilacuisine.ro/tort-cremsnit-cu-crema-de-vanilie-reteta-video/)
  uses **cremeș** for this same familiar childhood pastry. That is the one documented
  regional lexical equivalent in the graph batch, not evidence that all accepted
  forms are synonyms or that plain cremă means the completed pastry.
- **Tort Diplomat:** [the original2013 recipe](https://jamilacuisine.ro/tort-diplomat-facut-in-casa-reteta-video/)
  names pișcoturi, whipped cream, gelatin and oranges, and refrigerates the assembled
  dessert. [The manufacturer's independently developed version](https://www.oetker.ro/retete/r/tort-diplomat)
  also shows a pișcot structure, cream and gelatin. Fruit and structural choices vary;
  neither gelatin nor the specific fruit is asserted to occur in every conceivable
  recipe. The complete product is a concrete dessert, distinct from generic tort.
- **Pișcot:** [the original2016 preparation](https://www.barbatlacratita.ro/2016/03/piscoturi-de-casa.html)
  separates whites and yolks, uses flour, shapes elongated pieces, dusts them with
  powdered sugar and bakes them. It explicitly links their use to Diplomat, also
  corroborated by the separate cake recipes above. Shape, porosity and layered-dessert
  use distinguish the named product from a generic biscuit, though their ingredient
  overlap still needs an actual feedback/fairness check.
- **Ciocolată:** [the producer's solid chocolate](https://camarabucovinei.ro/products/ciocolata-de-casa)
  lists sugar. [The chef's original recipe](https://www.roberteisler.ro/retete/ciocolata-de-casa-1)
  explicitly adds **liquid milk** alongside milk powder, sets the mixture in a frame
  and cuts it into solid pieces. The milk association was not inferred from a drink.
  Existing cocoa, hazelnut and powdered-milk cues stay intact. The new links are
  variants: not all chocolate has added sugar or milk, and no brand identity changes.

These are source-based recognition judgments, not measured recall rates, Romanian
player interviews or proof of enjoyment. Familiar generic food names are not replaced
with obscure technical vocabulary to justify difficulty. All six raw records remain
subject to the existing playability and quality gates.

## Meanings that remain distinct or deferred

- **Chec/Pandișpan overlap is real.** Holding the latter avoids two forced promotions,
  but the Chec quality review still needs to test natural near guesses. Source-backed
  ingredients alone do not guarantee fair rank separation.
- **Brioșă is not in this raw batch.** The dictionary's cozonac-like brioche and modern
  muffin-style recipes have different leavening methods. The graph keeps those
  variants explicit; neither the word's native recognition nor five incoming links
  establishes a fair hidden target yet.
- **Biscuit/Pișcot are not full lexical equivalents.** Their family resemblance is
  expected. Each still needs clear actual openers, recovery and distinct intended
  answers; the target count alone proves none of these.
- **Ciocolată caldă remains its own beverage projection.** A milk ingredient in solid
  chocolate is not a reason to collapse that compound into the plain-food answer.
- **Cremă, tort, bicarbonat, bicarbonat de sodiu and gelatină** are not new aliases
  for a specific completed food or for the qualified culinary ingredients. Likewise
  powder, food bicarbonate, gelatin and yeast remain distinct.
- No alcohol-dependent premise, geographic exclusivity, nutrition superiority,
  storage-time claim or cooking-safety instruction is copied into a target.

The graph ingredients and named uses are factually acceptable for pending staging.
This document does not claim that indirect rankings, every natural guess, all prior
profiles or the final public selectors have passed; those require measured live
responses, the independent quality screen and the final bound release evidence.
