# ADR-0123: Extend preparation and cooling through specific reviewed concepts

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-08

## Context

V85 improves ingredient feedback but lacks ordinary preparation inputs such as whipped
cream and separated egg parts. Cooling feedback is weak and the distinction between
refrigeration and freezing is incomplete. New rounds need recognizable specific paths.

## Decision

Add nine independently reviewed preparation concepts, 30 accepted forms and 41 one-way
relations through the rollback-protected graph transaction. No old edge is removed.
Preserve existing owners and forms, qualify ambiguous tools/ingredients, and distinguish
variant recipes from universal claims. Preserve canonical Frigider; do not invent a concrete
object for the adjective “rece”. Candidate quantities never justify filler links.

Retire only Congelator's approximate Frigider projection. Its native identity now provides
the freezer-specific ice-cream association, while plain “mixer” retains its old projection.
The affected projected domain retains 18 rows; no vocabulary-floor change is required.
The other 468 projection rows and all four previous native audit tuples remain exact.

Measure native feedback and six-game impact before promoting separately reviewed playable
rounds through existing gates. Source-only ingredient concepts are not automatically valid
Contexto targets. Keep the current graph directions and qualified variant descriptions.

Use the existing closed exact-target feedback mechanism for two independently reviewed
reverse culinary associations: Ecler→Cremă de vanilie and Savarină→Frișcă. Their initial
rank/distance feedback was 322/4 and 140/4 despite the defining filling associations.
Both keep the submitted identity and return nonwinning hot feedback; exact target guesses
still win. Do not add reverse graph edges or affect feedback outside these two targets.
The four prior pairs, 71 legacy proxies and scoring formula remain unchanged.

Promote Brânză, Lapte, Savarină, Frișcă and Cremă de vanilie Contexto rounds plus
Frigider→Înghețată in Lanț after complete independent bound judgments. Keep the two
salience warnings explicitly justified and all eight historical pending holds unchanged.
Defer Biscuit and routes whose defining associations remain weak or imply a missing direct link.

## Acceptance

Both validators, the critique rubric, independent bound judgments, actual typed journeys,
exact historical reconstruction and the full integration matrix must pass. Record additions,
removals, changed profiles and remaining feedback problems separately. No rollout is implied.
