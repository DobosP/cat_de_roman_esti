Tobă and several savory targets and failed the ordinary topology floor. The naive
four-edge graph-only design added misleading warmth for geographic and historic targets
through transitive Baclava paths. Their raw captures remain `sim-one.json` and
`sim-four.json`. Those historical trials used provisional aliases; none should be read
as part of the final factual proposal, which keeps only `nucile`.
