# ADR-0074: Bound synonym expansion to typed games

Date: 2026-08-12
Status: superseded-by ADR-0075

## Decision

Add only independently unanimous, normalized-unique, exact same-referent forms to the
shared KG resolver, where typed Contexto and Lanț may use them. Put unanimously anchored
related/basic words only in Contexto's non-winning projection with rank penalty zero or
one. Defer every disagreement and reject accent-fold collisions or hard homonyms. Add no
nodes, edges, game records, hold dispositions, tap-game content, or derived boards in V50.
Preserve archived gate provenance while requiring new live Alchimie gates to bind the
current KG, rubric, runtime sources, generator, and source pack.

Supersede ADR-0068 only where its decision fixed the projection inventory at 453 terms;
the effective-scoring proxy, non-winning behavior, unique-target reserve, collision rules,
and all other V44 boundaries remain in force. The reviewed V50 inventory is 465 terms.

## Context / why

The V33 beginner benchmark already resolves all 322 eligible terms, and V44 established
the safe separation between exact aliases and approximate Contexto-only feedback. Adding
first-class concepts would reopen Lanț route widths, Alchimie recipe projections,
Contexto ranks, terminal puzzles, and derived-game source bindings without a measured
coverage need. Tap-first games do not consume typed aliases at all. The finite V50 funnel
therefore reviewed 50 missing surfaces with two independent lexical passes and no quota.

An alias-only KG release also exposed an archival boundary: V48's exact Alchimie evidence
must retain its historical full-file provenance even when unchanged topology receives new
aliases. Historical replay now verifies embedded digests and exact reproduced projection
rows; mutation still fails closed unless a live gate matches every current input.

## Consequences

The KG stays at 2,364 nodes, 9,217 edges and 180 puzzles, while aliases increase from
7,452 to 7,460. Contexto's non-winning projection increases from 453 to 465 terms across
the same 26 domains. Pack bytes and all 618 game records remain unchanged. Ranking and
derived wrapper metadata are rebound to the new KG, while their board arrays—including
the frozen 336-board derived payload—remain byte-stable. Any later topology wave, wild-
animal domain, projection-attempt identity, or disputed surface needs a separate review.
