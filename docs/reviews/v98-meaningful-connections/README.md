## Intended player outcomes

1. **Recognize useful everyday door vocabulary.** Review intrare, toc de ușă, balama and
   lemn as distinct concepts with truthful directed associations. Bare toc needs a sense
   review. None is a synonym or winning alias for ușă. Check spelling, diacritics, forms,
   no false wins, attempts and the feedback impact on prior targets before approval.
2. **Explain the Cacao→Lapte routes.** Four drafts describe cocoa variants, milk-based
   ice cream and cake served with milk. Bind each to its full edge snapshot, keep all
   five existing traversal directions and the three absent reverses, and show captions
   only for visible choices or earned progress. Both existing shortest routes must win.
3. **Give existing Alchimie dishes worthwhile continuations.** Supă de roșii and Mâncare
   de spanac currently have zero onward uses. Three research candidates reuse existing
   results and consume zero concept slots. Sources must support the exact prepared dish
   and transformation; do not install a weak recipe just to remove a terminal label.
4. **Build a small reserve of coherent playable rounds.** Scout familiar household
   candidates for Conexiuni, Intrusul, Perechi and the two route/target games. Prefer
   existing vocabulary, avoid V97 reskins and rejected inventory, and assess complete
   boards and natural selection. Ship only independently accepted candidates together.
   No quota overrides recognition, predicate honesty, uniqueness or playability.
