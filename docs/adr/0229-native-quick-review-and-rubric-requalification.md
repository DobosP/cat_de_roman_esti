## Qualification and limits

The exact historical/current tool reviews and actual focused checks remain separate from
operational Source27 results, dirty builds, portable compilation and final committed gates.
This ADR asserts no fresh Source27 factual/quality verdict or final current qualification.
Before qualification, complete actual proof/replay, whole native/historical checks, normal
managed HTTP, owning build/4i/unit/full on the exact final candidate SHA and disposable real
PostgreSQL RUN/PASS lanes. Default-suite 16 PG skips are classified pending obligations,
never PG qualification. Record operator, exact command, full source SHA and result hash.
A later receipt-only child must not be called the tested SHA. A changed candidate requires
fresh owning gates on its exact SHA; disclose product/source and receipt SHAs separately.
Keep qualification receipts in owned scratch when recording them would change that candidate.

Hashes/modes do not prove source truth, fairness, child safety, actor independence or resistance
to malicious Root/same-UID/kernel/container compromise. Recognition and enjoyment calibration
remain unmeasured. Preserve 79 held Conexiuni rows, 87 owner holds, six C9 guards and protected
803. Goldset937 remains UNRATIFIED; source-client M2 and two WikiTech policy URL questions
remain unresolved. DEX/Foundation stay denied for this run. No reset, source-policy permission,
production/account authorization, push grant or C31 class-G/KG admission follows.
