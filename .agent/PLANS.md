# ExecPlan contract

Each implementation ExecPlan is self-contained and begins with Branch, Session and Worktree.
Use these twelve sections in order:
1. Purpose / Big Picture
2. Progress (UTC timestamped checkboxes; checkbox lists only here)
3. Surprises & Discoveries (actual evidence)
4. Decision Log (decision, rationale, UTC date, verified version source)
5. Outcomes & Retrospective
6. Context and Orientation
7. Plan of Work
8. Concrete Steps (working directory and exact commands)
9. Validation and Acceptance (observable outputs and every required named gate check)
10. Idempotence and Recovery
11. Artifacts and Notes
12. Interfaces and Dependencies

Sections 2–5 stay current after every milestone. Record unavailable evidence honestly.
The ExecPlan cannot relax safety, content, privacy, CSP, a11y, provenance or result-integrity rules.
The configured app model/effort stays unchanged. SessionStart hooks are optional.
