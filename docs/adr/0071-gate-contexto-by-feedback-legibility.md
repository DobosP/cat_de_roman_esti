# ADR-0071: Gate Contexto targets by feedback legibility, not familiarity alone

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-01

## Context

V46 left 13 pending Cald sau Rece records. Every target cleared the engine's broad
reachability and responsive-zone floors, and most labels were familiar. Those aggregate
metrics hid release-breaking local behavior: several concrete targets had fewer than five
direct recognizable predecessors, `familie` made Mamă/Tată/Bunic/Bunică/Frate/Soră read
Înghețat while generic abstractions were hot, and the natural weather form `ploaia`
resolved to a served Cargo-song node. The rubric already names Sonicitate and
Recensământul populației as C1 failures.

V42's bindings are stale against the current KG and rubric. V44's exact gate covers two
different, already-approved targets. V47 therefore rebuilt all 13 dossiers and used two
fresh independent judgments. The deterministic critique reported zero failures and one
salience warning, proving that its floors are necessary but not sufficient.

## Decision

1. Bind the V47 gate to the exact 13-ID batch. Require two `promote` judgments; either
   `reject` rejects; otherwise synthesize `keep`.
2. Promote only `ct_gastronomie_300` (`Mâncare`). It passes C1-C6 with 15 recognizable
   direct predecessors, 2,259 responsive nodes, obvious openers, 19 existing target
   projections, and one existing feedback proxy.
3. Remove the ten exact records that fail C1, C2, C3, C6, lexical safety, or live gradient
   review. Familiarity, global reach, and a cluster of same-rank projections do not replace
   the five-neighbor C3 floor or an intuitive discovery field.
4. Keep `ct_meme_net_238` (`Shitpost`) and `ct_societate_257` (`Industrie`) pending and
   unserved. Profanity and the near-duplicate served `Industria românească` field trigger
   the mandatory ADR-0019/A5 owner boundary.
5. Add no alias, projection, proxy, node, or edge in this gate. Preserve the collision on
   `ploaia` honestly instead of stealing the song's surface. Any repair of Morcov,
   Familie, Farmacie, Ploaie, Frigider, or Creion requires a separately bound vocabulary
   or feedback-topology change and a fresh C1-C6 review.
6. Preserve every rejected record's exact dossier, digest, review binding, and final
   artifact as archive provenance because Contexto has no rejection ledger. Regenerate
   rankings and derived metadata in the same green change; preserve the frozen derived
   boards, KG, graph, sessions, scoring, hidden-answer boundary, and production deployment.

## Consequences

Cald sau Rece becomes 207 records = 205 approved + 2 pending and 202 unique selectable
targets. The original-game pack becomes 635 records = 609 approved + 26 pending, with 448
runtime-eligible records. The frozen derived catalog remains 336 boards (183 Intrusul /
153 Perechi) with unchanged boards-payload SHA-256
`71a2acefb7e0ec62da32ad2645238d73d5e83375808160c0bd1800febd3a73b6`.

The ten removed records cannot be mechanically blocked from reappearing under new IDs;
future Contexto authoring must consult the committed V47 archive and prove the named
failure changed. The two A5 holds require an explicit owner decision. No empty shelf or
common-word quota is evidence for promotion.
