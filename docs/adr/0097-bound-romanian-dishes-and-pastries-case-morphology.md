# ADR-0097: Bound Romanian dishes-and-pastries case morphology

Date: 2026-08-26
Status: accepted

## Decision

Add only independently unanimous, normalized-unique, natural genitive/dative forms of
existing Romanian dishes-and-pastries concepts to the shared typed resolver. V72 admits
all 50 forms from one fixed 25-owner funnel, with no rejection, deferral, projection,
node, edge, puzzle, game record, hold disposition, ranking row, or derived board.

Qualify both proposed `bulz` forms with `cu brânză` and both proposed `gogoașă` forms
with `prăjite`. Do not add their bare case forms in this wave: the qualifiers bind the
food concepts without claiming ordinary clod, lump, swelling, or lie senses.

Supersede ADR-0096 only where it fixed the resolver at 8,400 aliases and the fixture at
its V71 build identifier. Preserve its exact-alias and collision rules, inherited
70-surface nonaccepted ledger, two-surface actionable-fuzzy deny, topology, pack, frozen
ranking/derived payloads, sessions, privacy, and owner bounds. Preserve ADR-0093's
Contexto reserve/filtered-shelf weighting and ADR-0094's navigation contracts.

## Context / why

Existing gastronomy concepts for familiar Romanian dishes and pastries lacked bounded
forms such as `ciorbelor de perișoare`, `covrigilor de Buzău`, `salamurilor de biscuiți`,
and `tochiturilor moldovenești`. Two complete independent reviews agreed on the exact
owner for every surface, with no deferrals or projection additions.

The original bare `bulzului`/`bulzurilor` and `gogoșii`/`gogoșilor` proposals were not
safe enough for an exact shared resolver. Independent semantic audit required the food
qualifiers before the candidate freeze. The corrected 50-surface funnel has no exact,
projection, inherited-ledger, prior-review, beginner-benchmark, prior-owner, or wrong
fuzzy-owner collision; pre-application fuzzy resolution found 20 intended owners and
left 30 surfaces unresolved.

All 25 owners are existing `gastronomie` concepts with 4–23 incoming legal
non-distractor Lanț predecessors. The bounded alias wave therefore adds typed play
without inventing content or changing graph routes.

## Consequences

The alias-only application keeps the KG at 2,364 nodes, 9,217 edges, and 180 puzzles while
aliases increase from 8,400 to 8,450 under build
`fixture-v72-romanian-dishes-and-pastries-morphology`. Contexto's projection remains at
473 terms across 26 domains. Pack bytes, ranking rows, the frozen 336-board derived
payload, graph topology, sessions, accounts, frontend behavior, and deployment state
remain unchanged; only alias-bearing records and KG-bound wrapper metadata change.

Typed Contexto guesses and otherwise-legal Lanț hops gain all 50 reviewed case forms.
V71's exact `intrigii`/`intrigilor` actionable-fuzzy deny remains unchanged. Any further
paradigm, polyseme, projection, topology, or board wave needs a new finite review and test
contract.
