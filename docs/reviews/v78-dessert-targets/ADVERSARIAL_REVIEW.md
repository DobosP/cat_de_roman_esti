## Cozonac — drop before staging

C1, C2, C5 and C6 pass. Historic DEX/CADE/DLRLC records and current DOOM coverage establish durable recognition, while PRO TV ran a multi-round `Cozonacul de aur` contest in a general-audience 2025 show. The isolated noun overwhelmingly denotes the food; the fixed phrase `a dospi cozonacul` does not create a competing standalone target.

C4’s minimum also passes. `Făină` is rank 2/Fierbinte, `Desert` 4/Fierbinte, `Fruct` 17/Fierbinte, `Zahăr` 25/Cald and `Ou` 37/Cald. The V77 flour edge fixed one important route.

The refutation fails on whole-neighborhood legibility. Walnut is a defining Romanian cozonac filling, attested in the reviewed five-generation recipe, yet accepted `nucă` gets rank 504/Rece. Butter is rank 503/Rece. Generic `fruct` is rank 17/Fierbinte. The description itself foregrounds walnut and both major holidays, but `Crăciun` and `Paște` are unresolved. A player reasoning from the specific filling or festive identity is pushed away from an easy target while a generic category word is rewarded. The 23 direct incoming neighbors satisfy the count floor, but the actual route behavior does not satisfy C3’s “legible” half.

## Clătite — drop before staging

C1, C2, C5 and C6 pass. DLRLC defines the dish and supplies literary usage; AGERPRES and 2024–2025 national TV independently use it in familiar recipe contexts. The standalone plural under `gastronomie` dominantly names the dish. The same written form can be a participial/adjectival form of `a clăti`, but that reading normally needs an expressed noun and does not create a competing standalone entity here.

This candidate has the stronger formal C4 case: `Făină` ranks 2, `Desert` 3, `Ou` 5 and `Dulceață` 7, all Fierbinte; `Lapte` is rank 40/Cald. Nine direct predecessors are recognizable.

The gem path still defeats the easy-round case. DEX defines gem as a fruit preserve cooked with sugar. Romanian clătite sources present `gem` and `dulceață` as alternative fillings, including both terms in the same menu context. Nevertheless, accepted `gem` is six hops/rank 1699/Inghetat while `Dulceață` is one hop/rank 7/Fierbinte. Butter and honey also score six hops/Inghetat. This is more harmful than an unknown obscure input: the service accepts a conventional filling and returns confident “frozen” feedback. Several good openers do not make that contradiction fair for `usor`.

A later repair should preserve gem and dulceață as distinct food guesses while making their clătite associations comparably legible. Treating `gem` as an exact alias/win for Dulceață would erase a real lexical distinction and is outside this review.
