## Existing words repaired

Biscuit gains flour, butter, sugar, oven, baking powder and food bicarbonate cues.
The original manufacturer recipe directly lists all five ingredients and bakes the
cut dough. Flour is a general ingredient core; butter, sugar and the two leaveners
are explicitly recipe/sort variants. This does not call every savory cracker sweet
or every biscuit a butter biscuit. A dessert-category hub was not added just to
make a broad opener hot. Its actual graded feedback must be measured after apply.

Existing Ciocolată gains sugar and liquid-milk associations. The latter is supported
by a chef's **solid homemade chocolate**: liquid milk is used as well as milk powder,
the mixture sets in a frame, and it is cut into pieces. It is not a link to the
separate hot beverage. Both new edges use variant wording; neither every chocolate
nor every dark chocolate must contain milk or added sugar.

Native `chec` and `brioșă` should retire only their own bread-based projection rows.
Keep other bread cluster surfaces, the separate `ciocolată caldă` projection and
all existing native-audit identities intact. Count the two exact native upgrades
through the already accepted coverage policy, without inventing filler vocabulary.

## Recipe overlap and honest target holds

**Chec and Pandișpan do not have disjoint recipe definitions.** The explicitly
included Bucate Aromate source calls its chec a pandișpan and uses no baking powder.
Neither our descriptions nor the edge labels claim that all chec uses powder or
all pandișpan excludes it. The authored sets capture documented preparations and
uses, not a fabricated categorical boundary. The Pandișpan target is held beside
Chec until an independent player-oriented review can justify distinguishing it.

Pișcot and Pandișpan legitimately share flour and separated egg components. Their
shapes, textures and uses differ, but recipe overlap alone cannot establish a fair
hidden-word distinction. Pișcot's documented sugar dusting and Diplomat use supply
additional meaning. Its raw review must probe expected pastry/recipe guesses rather
than assuming five incoming edges prove uniqueness or enjoyment.

**Brioșă also has documented breadth.** The dictionary describes a small cozonac-like
brioche, while the manufacturer calls its baking-powder chocolate cakes brioșe.
The original GroupeSEB book supplies the yeast variant. These are variant-specific
edges, not a yeast/powder substitution rule. No `muffin` synonym is asserted. Keep its
hidden target held until C5 and actual feedback are judged; native recognition as an
ordinary food word is not itself a promotion decision.

The proposed six-word target screen is therefore Biscuit, Chec, Cremșnit,
Tort Diplomat, Pișcot and existing Ciocolată. These are recommendations for separate
raw factual/quality review and bound dossiers, not approved or embedded pack records.
Additional Lanț or Alchimie rounds require real route/recipe profiles and independent
review; no quota is filled with a taxonomy-only bridge or a recipe clone.

## Relation and input boundaries

All 50 records preserve their authored direction. Six outgoing relationships are
specific serving, preparation-base, type or dough-family claims: Chec→Lapte,
Pandișpan→Amandină, Cremșnit→Prăjitură, Tort Diplomat→Desert,
Pișcot→Tort Diplomat and Brioșă→Cozonac. No ingredient arrow was reversed to inflate
incoming target counts. Product-type links are honest classifications supporting
navigation, not a new catch-all food hub.

Praf de copt, sodium bicarbonate, gelatin and existing Drojdie remain distinct.
Bicarbonate is one component in a documented baking-powder formula; this does not
make the powders interchangeable. Bicarbonat de amoniu, sodium carbonate, agar and
pectin are not alternate aliases. Bicarbonate and gelatin aliases retain the food
qualifier; bare `bicarbonat`, `bicarbonat de sodiu`, `gelatină`, `tort` and `rece`
are not newly given these native identities.

The `cremeș` equivalent is explicitly used for the same pastry in the original
2024 JamilaCuisine article and discussed as a regional name by a linguistic
publisher. Its spelling does not capture the separate bare `cremă` input or the
existing Cremă de vanilie identity. Plural/definite forms remain bound to their own
food rather than being treated as new concepts.

Gelatin's four uses are named variants. Traditional piftie can set from its own
collagen without an added packet; ordinary whipped cream and most ice cream also
need no added gelatin. The labels describe demonstrated stabilizing/gelling variants,
not universal recipes. Storage durations, medical claims, marketing superiority,
food-safety instructions and alcohol-based gameplay are not imported from sources.

## Original source evidence

Every edge index is mapped to source IDs in the exact proposal. Sources were
inspected as original pages or their indexed original text; no recipe content from
search-result forums or generated summaries is used as an authoring instruction.
Only the narrow claim summarized below is carried into the graph.

- **biscuit:** [Original manufacturer recipe explicitly includes flour, butter, sugar, baking powder and food-grade sodium bicarbonate, then bakes the cut biscuits. Variant ingredients are not universal requirements.](https://www.oetker.ro/retete/r/biscuiti-cu-unt-love-letters)

- **chec:** [Original2013 recipe includes flour, sugar, egg, oil, cocoa and baking powder; author also explicitly serves slices with milk. Cocoa and leavener are recipe variants.](https://jamilacuisine.ro/chec-pufos-cu-cacao-reteta-video/)

- **chec_overlap:** [Author calls this chec a pandișpan and uses no baking powder. This refutes any absolute chec/pandișpan or powder/no-powder boundary.](https://bucate-aromate.ro/2019/07/chec-cu-visine-si-cacao/)

- **pandispan:** [Original recipe separates egg whites and yolks, combines with sugar/flour and bakes. It explicitly has no baking powder; that observation is for this variant, not every recipe carrying the name.](https://www.lauralaurentiu.ro/retete-culinare/prajituri-torturi/pandispan-cu-prune-reteta-video-pentru-o-prajitura-pufoasa-gustoasa-si-economica.html)

- **pandispan_amandina:** [Original Amandină preparation describes its cocoa sponge as pandișpan; the outgoing base-for relation explicitly names the cocoa variant.](https://jamilacuisine.ro/prajitura-amandina-amandine-facute-in-casa/)

- **cremsnit:** [Original2014 recipe uses pastry sheets, vanilla cream and starch; it explicitly calls the finished food a prăjitură.](https://jamilacuisine.ro/prajitura-cremsnit-pas-cu-pas-reteta-video/)

- **cremsnit_cold:** [Original recipe uses vanilla cream between pastry sheets, chills the assembled pastry in the refrigerator and dusts it with powdered sugar.](https://bucate-aromate.ro/2025/10/cremsnit-cu-aluat-de-casa/)

- **cremes_name:** [Original2024 article uses cremșnit and cremeș for the same pastry. This supports a regional lexical equivalent, not a new separate concept.](https://jamilacuisine.ro/tort-cremsnit-cu-crema-de-vanilie-reteta-video/)

- **diplomat:** [Original recipe uses pișcoturi, whipped cream, gelatin and oranges and sets the assembled dessert in the refrigerator. These claims describe its actual variant.](https://jamilacuisine.ro/tort-diplomat-facut-in-casa-reteta-video/)

- **piscot:** [Original2016 recipe separates whites/yolks, adds flour, shapes elongated pieces, dusts with powdered sugar and bakes. It uses pișcoturi in Tort Diplomat.](https://www.barbatlacratita.ro/2016/03/piscoturi-de-casa.html)

- **briosa_quick:** [Manufacturer recipe calls these quick cake-like pieces brioșe and explicitly includes flour, butter, baking powder and chocolate pieces.](https://www.oetker.ro/retete/r/briose-cu-bucatele-de-ciocolata)

- **briosa_yeast:** [Original GroupeSEB recipe booklet has a Brioșe recipe with yeast. This is a different preparation variant, not evidence that yeast and baking powder are interchangeable.](https://dam.groupeseb.com/m/5111943d0aede5fe/original/NC00126318-Recipe.pdf)

- **briosa_meaning:** [DEX describes brioșă as a small pastry from cozonac-like dough; this supports the qualified outgoing dough-family relation and flags modern muffin/brioche sense breadth.](https://dexonline.ro/intrare/brio%C8%99%C4%83/6859)

- **powder:** [Manufacturer lists sodium bicarbonate with other leavening components in a baking-powder variant. Bicarbonate is one constituent, not an alias or complete substitute.](https://www.oetker.ro/produsele/p/praf-de-copt-pentru-air-fryer)

- **bicarbonat_turta:** [Original2015 gingerbread recipe explicitly uses sodium bicarbonate.](https://jamilacuisine.ro/turta-dulce-glazurata-reteta-video/)

- **bicarbonat_mici:** [Original2025 mici recipe explicitly uses food sodium bicarbonate. No historical-origin or health claim is imported.](https://jamilacuisine.ro/mici-de-casa-pasta-pentru-mici-suculenti-si-deliciosi-reteta-video/)

- **gelatina_frisca:** [Original cream-equipment supplier preparation adds gelatin to stabilize whipped cream; the graph labels only this stabilized variant.](https://exoticwhip.com/ro/blog-ro/reteta-de-frisca-stabilizata/)

- **gelatina_ice:** [Original2008 melon ice-cream recipe incorporates gelatin into the base before freezing. No health superiority, storage-time or preparation-safety advice is imported.](https://teoskitchen.ro/2008/07/inghetata-de-pepene-galben.html)

- **gelatina_piftie:** [Original2013 chicken-wing piftie recipe explicitly adds gelatin. This is not a claim that all traditional piftie needs added gelatin.](https://www.delicatesa.ro/retete/piftie-din-aripioare-de-pui-2728)

- **chocolate_sugar:** [Producer names sugar among ingredients of its solid homemade chocolate. Only ingredient presence is used, not claimed geographic exclusivity or nutritional benefit.](https://camarabucovinei.ro/products/ciocolata-de-casa)

- **chocolate_milk:** [Original chef recipe directly uses liquid milk as well as milk powder, sets the mixture in a frame and cuts it into solid chocolate pieces. This is not the hot-drink sense.](https://www.roberteisler.ro/retete/ciocolata-de-casa-1)

- **cremsnit_lexical:** [Linguistic article records the DOOM plural cremșnituri and describes cremeș as a regional name. The recipe source independently attests that name equivalence.](https://editurajunimea.ro/reviste/revista-scriptor/foaie-foi-foitaj/)

- **pandispan_lexical:** [Dictionary records pandișpan and its plural pandișpanuri; no claim that the category has a disjoint recipe boundary from chec.](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/pandi%C8%99pan/27977)
