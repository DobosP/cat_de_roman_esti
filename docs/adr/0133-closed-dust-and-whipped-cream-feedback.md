# ADR-0133: Improve specific dust and whipped-cream cues without broad retargeting

- Status: partially superseded by ADR-0135 for Praf ownership/fallback
- Date: 2026-09-08

## Context

V88's source correction leaves Tort Diplomat→Frișcă at rank345/cold even though
whipped cream is a defining component of the dessert. Its unrelated Zacuscă control
is warm through a truthful kitchen/fridge path; deleting those edges would corrupt
other games. New household candidates also expose Praf's broad Pământ projection:
it is far from familiar dustpan, mop and vacuum targets despite their cleaning use.

## Decision

Add exactly one native nonwinning feedback pair: Tort Diplomat for hidden Frișcă.
Original recipe sources independently support that specific association. Do not
extend it to Cremșnit or Pișcot, restore fermented-smântână equivalence, remove true
kitchen/storage edges or blacklist Zacuscă to force a desired rank.

Add exactly one projected neighborhood for Praf, containing the three explicit
Făraș, Mop and Aspirator targets. Preserve its public ID, existing Pământ fallback,
landscape domain and rank penalty everywhere else; no direct-neighbor expansion.
Dictionary and original manufacturer sources support dust collection for each,
including ordinary microfiber mops. Other earth/powder words cannot inherit it.

Both changes use existing closed feedback mechanisms and keep related guesses
nonwinning. Rank2 is an editorial association judgment, not a source-derived numerical
fact or a synonym claim. Exact target input still wins, and private-answer/typo
filtering keeps its current contract. No graph edge, concept, alias, projection term,
scoring formula, session field, resource limit or 71-node proxy mapping changes.

Promote only the reviewed Mop household round. Reject the staged Făraș and Aspirator
records: their three recognizable direct predecessors fail the five-neighbor floor
in [ADR-0071](0071-gate-contexto-by-feedback-legibility.md), despite five neighbors when
both directions are combined. Mop has five incoming cues. Preserve the rejected
records, exact dossiers and both final judgments; the valid Praf feedback scopes do
not grant hidden-target approval or count as graph predecessors. No graph padding.

## Acceptance and limits

Require exact independent proposal/source review, actual post-change private/public
journeys, all-target isolation and repeat/resume/exact-win/privacy controls. Compare
old-target responses on unchanged graph bytes. Existing pastry and household ranking
costs outside these four word/target scopes remain visible. Household round approval
still requires its own full rubric, bound dossiers and independent promotion judgments.
Evidence is in `docs/reviews/v89-feedback-and-conexiuni-recovery/`.

## Partial supersession

[ADR-0135](0135-household-discovery-concepts.md) replaces the projected Praf identity and
its three-target neighborhood with a native dust owner and reviewed direct graph uses.
The native Tort Diplomat→Frișcă pair remains accepted and unchanged. The original
V89 proposal, implementation and projection-only tests remain historical evidence.
