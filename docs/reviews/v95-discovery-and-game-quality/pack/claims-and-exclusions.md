## Proposed play and explicit caveats

Conexiuni adds nine previously unused words in that game: Orez, Fasole, Mazăre, Arpacaș, Unt, Miere, Dulceață, Lampă and Lingură. The final quartet can be found by the Romanian word *coadă* across an animal, fruit stalk, utensil handle and instrument form. The exact label **Pot avea coadă** deliberately allows a grand piano without falsely claiming upright pianos have that shape. Its other groups are boiled food grains, familiar bread spreads and light sources. All sixteen stored identities are concepts; recognition and the shared-word step, rather than node-type sorting or obscure proper names, carry the board. The other three groups are meant as anchors. Arpacaș is the least everyday member, but recognizable from ordinary cooking/colivă and supported by three obvious grains. The light group intentionally includes both a lamp and a bulb: they satisfy the same predicate at different assembly sizes, without any mirrored group elsewhere. The stored Salată de vinete description explicitly denotes the cooked spread; this is not a claim about raw eggplants. “Boabe fierte în mâncăruri” includes cereal grains and legume seeds under their shared culinary form, not an assertion that all four are cereals. Phrases describing arbitrary physical possibilities do not establish alternative groups: boiling a cherry or spreading crushed rice is not the normal use named by these categories. Reviewers must still assess the partition, difficulty and potential loose-wordplay issue directly; zero lints is not a quality verdict.

Penar is the stronger Cald sau Rece target. Five recognizable native predecessors pass the floor, and actual school, schoolbag, pupil and contents guesses are warm/hot: școală rank 2, ghiozdan 4, elev 10, stilou/radieră 11, creion/pix/caiet 39. Its displayed definition is an ordinary container for writing tools used by pupils.

Clește has exactly five recognizable native predecessors (A lucra, Cui, Șurub, Ciocan, Șurubelniță). Actual ciocan/șurubelniță rank 3, cui/șurub 10 and a lucra 2. The tool has one dominant standalone everyday reading despite the related crab-claw sense. However, the broader experience has real unresolved vocabulary debt: **unealtă and fier are unknown**, atelier only rank 99/lukewarm, and foarfecă rank 223/lukewarm. Unknown guesses do not consume attempts. The five hot neighboring workshop guesses make this a viable proposal, but this is the weakest candidate and should be withheld if the independent quality review regards the missing category noun as disqualifying. No graph or alias changes were made to rescue it. All failures remain in `broad-opener-probes.json`.

The first easy Lanț journey connects an oven to a crumb via either bread or a biscuit. Both two-hop routes have everyday actions and separate intermediates: bake → leave little pieces when cut/broken/eaten. The second connects powdered sugar to Tort Diplomat through whipped cream or ladyfinger biscuits. Both routes are variant-aware: powdered sugar sweetens some whipped cream and coats some ladyfinger recipes; cream is a cake ingredient and ladyfingers line some versions. Existing captions explicitly say “unele variante” where appropriate. The target description allows sponge cake OR ladyfingers and calls the dessert chilled, airy and fruity; this agrees with original recipes. Neither journey rests on a generic geographic or taxonomic shortcut. Four author BFF replays win, and all four endpoints are new to Lanț. Endpoint descriptions and every path caption were checked, not only the names.

Paraphrases only. Dictionary entries are original named dictionary text mirrored by dexonline; product/recipe/manual pages are original publisher/manufacturer evidence. No contemporary price, rule or health advice enters the game.
