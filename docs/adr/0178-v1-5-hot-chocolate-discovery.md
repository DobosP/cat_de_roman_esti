# ADR-0178: V1.5 hot-chocolate discovery

Date: 2026-10-06
Status: accepted; independently reviewed, fully verified and landed locally e8c4bbc

## Context

The owner requested that V1.5 be fixed and landed. The two i02 shared-graph directions
remain held: the bounded easy-game search found no new usable route, and independent
simple-path enumeration also found none in the two affected authored Lanț records.
Their reachability, weighted-feedback and latent-recipe effects do not establish a
player benefit. Those findings and the original source evidence remain preserved.

The existing i06 research offered a narrower addition: a world-local hot-chocolate
recipe using already earned Milk and Chocolate. The original Kaufland recipe supports
heated milk and chocolate as its foundation, with optional additional ingredients.
The existing Contexto projection already recognizes the phrase through Ceai; it remains
unchanged. This is a new exploration discovery, not a new shared-graph identity or alias.

## Decision

V1.5 adds one world-local concept, `alw_food_ciocolata_calda`, and one recipe,
`ciocolata-calda-lapte-ciocolata`. Its description and explanation retain the heated-milk
variant qualification. The world grows from 251 concepts/351 recipes/147 discoveries
to 252/352/148. Eight starters, 96 supplies, twelve unlock tiers, 32 optional goals,
all existing recipe results and every earlier earned item remain intact.

Source5 names the exact reviewed Source4 parent. World uses Source5; unchanged Quick
and Extensions retain their Source4 authorship and receive fresh installed audits and
same-role final reviews against the completed runtime. Reserve remains unchanged.
The existing strict native transactions, source/export/authority gates, independent
reference captures, historical assertions and final assembled review govern landing.

The installed-authority operator reconstructs an already installed world through a
private phase that requires its generated world ID, mechanics and complete compatibility
history to equal the installed world. Public candidate/proposal staging still requires
the immutable predecessor to be current. Exact candidate bytes, raw judgments, fresh
final audit/reviews and actual installed artifact checks remain mandatory. This fixes
the first installed recipe transition without weakening the staging boundary.

The authored legacy Python world source remains historical. Genuine pinned historical
loader contexts preserve its old literals; current Python serving/reference capture
reads the generated current world. No generated fixture or golden response is edited
by hand. All earlier native sources, corpora and full-byte inverses are retained.

## Evidence and consequences

The exact candidate, independent raw reviews, guarded transaction and native evidence
are in [the V1.5 review](../reviews/v1-5-hot-chocolate/README.md). Prospective validation
completed all 33 goal modes at 148 discoveries, ten compatibility books and 1,156
historical saved prefixes. A 60-request native HTTP journey proves ordinary earning,
the new discovery, old/new save restoration, repeats, hints, privacy and service isolation.

Completion expands from 147 to 148 discoveries. Existing completed goals and earned
progress remain; the additional earned discovery may advance a later count-based unlock.
Transient empty-pair observations are session-local, so a fresh service restores saved
discoveries and can retry a previously empty pair. No hot reload or human enjoyment
measurement is claimed. Shared concepts, synonyms, forms, graph links, curated rounds
and their eligible/preferred pools do not grow in this version.

The strict mixed-source authority and source/reference gates are qualified. All required integrated gates pass. Exact-revision
assembled acceptance still precedes local landing. Push and deployment remain separately authorized. After qualified local
landing and verified-merged cleanup, the owner-authorized loop starts V1.6.
