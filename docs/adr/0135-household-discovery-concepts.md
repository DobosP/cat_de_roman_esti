# ADR-0135: Native household debris and truthful directed discovery

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-08

## Context

V89's household screen found ordinary crumbs and lint unrecognized and treated Praf
through an earth approximation. Its Făraș/Aspirator candidates were correctly rejected
for only three incoming neighbors. Their union adjacency counts had hidden the deficit.
Original dictionaries and manufacturers support broader dust, crumb and textile-fibre
meanings and specific collection/care relations independently of any desired round count.

## Decision

Add exactly the independently reviewed Praf, Firimitură and Scamă concepts, their ten
stored grammatical forms and seventeen one-way non-distractor links. Use source-qualified
labels for microfiber dust collection, some carpets' fibre shedding, corded/battery
vacuum power and surface-appropriate dish-sponge use. Strengths0.70–0.90 are editorial
weights, not physical measurements. No synonym, inferred reverse link, existing-node
correction or existing-edge removal is part of this graph batch. The graph preflight's
four-incident/two-same-category floor does not approve a hidden Contexto target.

Make ordinary dust native under `n_v90_household_praf`: add only `praf` to the existing
native surface exclusion set and remove the now-unused three-target projected Praf
neighborhood. Retire synthetic ID `ctxp_81703160cad7893fa1c9` and its Pământ fallback;
retain every other464 projection row,26 domains,71 legacy proxies,11 native exact pairs
and four audit-only native tuples. Native direct graph feedback replaces the old scoped
rank2 exception; no new scoring override or formula change is authorized by this choice.

Praf/praful/prafului share one native identity and deduplicated attempt. They remain
nonwinning for cleaning-tool targets. Praf de copt, Lapte praf, Pământ and other powders
retain their separate owners and meanings. Do not add prafuri as an automatic dust form.
Custom graphs without the new native owner no longer receive the retired synthetic dust
fallback, following the existing static native-exclusion contract. This does not introduce
a session-upgrade protocol across process restarts.

Stage only the two raw quality keeps, Făraș and Aspirator, after graph application and
actual migration verification; allocate fresh IDs through the supported importer. Their
new incoming counts are5 and8, with useful dust/crumb/carpet/power approaches, but final
C1–C6 dossiers and two independent promotion judgments remain mandatory. Keep the V89
rejected IDs352/354 and all original judgments as history; do not retroactively approve them.

Drop Burete de vase from this raw batch despite its six numeric incoming neighbors:
its Masă-based plate proxy and floor/washing shortcuts still weaken dish-specific
legibility. Retain the truthful plate/water graph relations. Hold the explored Conexiuni
and Lanț stock for actual novelty or weak alternative paths; do not pad their predicates
or add an incidental recipe. None of the three new concepts has enough incoming neighbors
to be proposed as a hidden target itself.

## Acceptance and consequences

Independent factual and quality reviews bind the exact3-node/17-link/10-form payload and
disclose source redirects, alternate original documents and qualified inferences. The
Praf migration also requires exact implementation, identity/attempt, clue/resume, win,
typo privacy, missing-owner and all-target impact checks. Compare all240 old approved
Contexto records with the declared70-word sample and preserve every measured cost.

The truthful Farfurie→Burete edge also opens exactly seven existing kitchen concepts
(Oală, Tigaie, Cană, Castron, Farfurie, Furculiță, Lingură) through the older cleaning
bridge. Six legacy proxy nodes could reach the old mature target set before; thirteen
can now. Removing only applied edge de8797 restores the old six-node set. The71 scorer
mappings stay byte-identical. Common-target reachability grows2343→2353: three new nodes
and these seven old nodes. This is a deliberate topology change, not seven new concepts
or a promise of uniform feedback improvement. Retain exact isolation/history tests and
compare every old recipe/route before accepting final integration.

Run supported graph/import/promotion writes serially, regenerate coherent rubric/KG/pack
sidecars and mobile metadata, preserve all old approved records,83 Alchimie recipe books,
100 Lanț profiles/menus and336 derived boards, and complete independent review/full gates
before local landing. Sessions, anonymous production and their resource limits remain.
