### `contexto[0]` — Cornulețe — factual accept

RRI gives a familiar fragile pastry filled with ground walnut and plum jam, baked and rolled in powdered sugar. Current AGERPRES coverage independently lists `cornulețe cu gem` among Romanian regional recipes. ### `contexto[1]` — Gogoși — factual accept

DEX/DLRLC define a gogoașă as leavened dough fried in fat or oil and commonly dusted with sugar. RRI calls gogoși a popular dessert, gives an oil-fried recipe and records serving with gem or honey. Current PRO TV independently shows the ordinary family preparation fried in hot oil and another current article records jam as a normal serving or filling option.

This supports both exact feedback claims. Ulei is central to the standard fried preparation; Gem is a familiar variant rather than a universal ingredient. Baked or unfilled gogoși remain ordinary variants, so the policy must express useful proximity rather than necessity. All seven direct target relations are plausible dessert, fried-dough, street-food or fair-food associations. The figurative plural `gogoși` can mean lies and the noun can name cocoons or swellings, but those senses normally need phrase/context; the food reading dominates within gastronomy. Easy-band fairness still requires actual probes.

### `contexto[2]` — Telemea — factual accept

DEX defines telemea as cheese made from drained curd and preserved in brine. The EU specification for Telemea de Ibănești describes salting and maturation in brine at 21–23% salt concentration, while an EU implementing regulation records about 24,000 tonnes of Romanian Telemea production in 2014. Current Romfilatelia/AGERPRES coverage independently presents Telemea de Ibănești as a representative Romanian cheese and again identifies brine maturation.

Sare→Telemea is therefore a defining association, although salt concentration and perceived saltiness vary among products. The ten direct non-distractor relations to Brânză, Mămăligă, Bulz, Plăcinte, other Romanian cheeses and serving contexts are factual. Telemea is a concrete, unambiguous food word; actual evidence must still justify the easy band.

The six direct non-distractor relations—Cartof, Mâncare stradală, Mici, Șaorma, Air fryer and Șnițel—are factual. The compound has one dominant concrete food sense and is suitable for later easy-band review.
